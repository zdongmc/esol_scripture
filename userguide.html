Scripture Alphabet Guide

A one-page tool that prints one Bible verse in up to 17 languages side by side, so every learner in the room reads the same words in their own language.

zdongmc.github.io/esol_scripture/
Scan to open
1

Open the page

Scan the code, or type the address into any browser. Nothing to install, no sign-in.

2

Pick a letter

Twenty-five letters, A to Z — there is no V. All are listed below.

3

English version

Good News (default), Easy-to-Read for newer readers, or Berean Standard. Headings never change.

4

Choose the languages

All 17 start ticked. Untick what your class does not need — a shorter list prints in larger type.

5

Print or save a PDF

Press Print / Save as PDF, or save a PDF to email ahead. Only the handout prints.
